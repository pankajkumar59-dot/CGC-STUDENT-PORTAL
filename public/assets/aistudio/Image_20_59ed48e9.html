Here is Peterson's Solution simplified: A classic software-based solution to the critical-section problem restricted to two processes alternating execution.

Algorithmic Pillars
1
Shared Memory Variables: Uses int turn (whose turn to enter) and boolean flag[2] (process readiness).
2
Mutual Exclusion Guaranteed: Only one process enters the Critical Section at any instant.
3
Progress & Bounded Waiting: No deadlock occurs; a process will not wait indefinitely to gain entry.
