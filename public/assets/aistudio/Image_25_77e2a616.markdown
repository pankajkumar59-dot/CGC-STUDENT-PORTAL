## Brand & Style
The design system delivers an ambitious, energetic, and authoritative campus experience for students, faculty, and prospective scholars. It balances institutional legacy with cutting-edge academic innovation, projecting academic rigor, community vibrancy, and structured clarity.

The visual style blends modern corporate clarity with tactile collegiate accents. The interface avoids bureaucratic clutter, opting instead for crisp surfaces, structured typographic contrast, and deliberate bursts of varsity-inspired amber gold. Interactions feel responsive and confident, engineered for rapid contextual transitions—from glancing at real-time lecture timetables during a sprint across the campus quad to deep reading digital study notes and course syllabi in the library.

## Colors
The palette grounds itself in collegiate heritage while using vibrant energy to drive motivation and hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#1B365D`)**: Deep Heritage Navy. Serves as the bedrock of navigation bars, primary calls-to-action, high-emphasis text, and core institutional markers.
- **Secondary (`#F5A623`)**: Vibrant Amber Gold. Reserved for high-value interactive highlights, attendance progress indicators, pending alerts, and varsity badges.
- **Tertiary (`#2B7A78`)**: Campus Spruce / Verdigris. Employed for verified academic status, passing grades, laboratory modules, and active session pills.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate Gray. Drives balanced secondary copy, structural dividers, and subtle inactive state styling.

Canvas and card surfaces rely on a crisp foundation: pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary elevated cards, backed by layered architectural washes of Slate Ice (`#F8FAFC`) and Muted Pergola (`#EDF2F7`).

## Typography
The system employs an authoritative geometric pairing:

- **Headings & Key Accents (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers optimistic, modern character through rounded geometries, balanced counters, and tight tracking for high-impact campus branding and course titles.
- **Reading & System Data (Inter)**: Provides pristine legibility across high-density reading surfaces, such as exam regulations, downloadable syllabus sheets, and research paper summaries.

Numerical values (marks, attendance percentages, room codes) utilize tabular figures to maintain horizontal alignment within data rows and schedule calendars.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive 4-column mobile grid scalable to an 8-column configuration on small tablets. Content conforms fluidly to screen width within device-safe margins.

- **Vertical Cadence**: Built on an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid for badge typography alignment and micro-tag spacing.
- **Horizontal Screen Padding**: Mobile screens enforce a 16px (`1rem`) outer margin to maximize usable horizontal space for dense course timetables, expanding to 24px (`1.5rem`) on tablets.
- **Content Blocks**: Cards and list groups use a uniform 16px (`1rem`) spacing gap (`space-md`) to guarantee optical separation without fragmenting information density.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on crisp surface layering combined with ambient blue-tinted shadows to prevent dirty gray washouts:

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Background wash (`#F8FAFC`) with no shadow. Used for base screens and unselected list rows.
- **Level 1 (Card Default)**: `0 1px 3px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.04)`. Outlined by a subtle 1px border (`#EDF2F7`). Applied to syllabus items, profile rows, and timetable blocks.
- **Level 2 (Elevated / Floating Cards)**: `0 4px 14px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.04)`. Applied to interactive cards, date selectors, and active lecture alerts.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Overlays & Persistent Navigation)**: `0 10px 25px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.12), 0 4px 10px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.06)`. Used for bottom sheets, quick-action menus, and modal dialogs.
- **Accent Glow**: `0 0 12px rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.35)`. Reserved strictly for live academic alerts, upcoming assignment reminders, and highlighted study tags.

## Shapes
The visual identity applies modern, friendly corner radiuses balanced against scholarly structure:

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px)**: Input fields, list tiles, and secondary buttons.
- **Containers & Cards (1rem / 16px)**: Timetable cards, grade overviews, and academic event modals.
- **Feature Modules (1.5rem / 24px)**: Top-level hero banners, attendance status cards, and bottom sheet containers.
- **Pill Badges & Meta-tags (Full rounded radius / 9999px)**: Academic badges, classroom tags, status indicators, and floating filter chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Navy (`#1B365D`) fill, white text, 8px border radius, 48px standard touch height. Active states shift to an intense ink tint (`#0F2537`).
- **Accent / Action**: Vibrant Amber (`#F5A623`) fill with deep navy text, reserved for "Submit Assignment," "Register for Fest," or "Pay Fee."
- **Secondary / Ghost**: White or transparent background with 1.5px border in `#EDF2F7` or `#1B365D`, paired with `#1B365D` text.

### Chips & Study Pills
- **Academic Badges**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `4px 12px`. Light-tinted backgrounds: amber tint (`#FEF6E9`) with `#C57A07` text for pending tasks; spruce tint (`#E8F4F3`) with `#2B7A78` text for cleared credits.
- **Filter Chips**: 32px height, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), active state converts to full navy fill with white text.

### Cards
- Standard modules leverage pure white background surfaces wrapped in a 1px border of `#EDF2F7`, 16px rounded corners, and Level 1 elevation.
- Hero modules (e.g., Attendance Meter) feature subtle vertical linear gradients from deep navy to vibrant slate, utilizing white typography and high-contrast amber tracking rings.

### Lists & Academic Feeds
- Syllabi and lecture schedules display divided list modules separated by 1px divider lines (`#F1F5F9`).
- Left-hand accent tracks (4px wide, vertical border radius) visually distinguish subjects (e.g., Engineering, Business Studies, Computer Science) using designated color accents.

### Form Inputs
- Single-line fields: 48px height, 8px border radius, `#F8FAFC` fill, `#CBD5E1` border.
- Focus state activates a 1.5px primary navy border (`#1B365D`) paired with an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.12)` halo ring.

### Campus-Specific Extras
- **Semester Timeline Indicator**: Horizontal scrolling date-and-class indicator pairing weekday initials with single-tap lecture jump points.
- **CGPA & Attendance Ring**: Radial micro-progress meter showcasing threshold margins (e.g., 75% mandatory attendance warnings).
